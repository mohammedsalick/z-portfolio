Pernambut, Tamil Nadu Active Political Leadership · 2025

Aaliyar Zubair Ahmed is a prominent political leader and dedicated public servant in Pernambut, Tamil Nadu. As the Chairman and Town Secretary of DMK Pernambut, he plays a pivotal role in grassroots political organization and public welfare initiatives across the region.

Widely recognized as a strong voice of the people, he works tirelessly for development, social justice, and community upliftment — his leadership grounded in empathy, integrity, and an unwavering commitment to the residents of Pernambut.

A successful entrepreneur, he manages AAB Vegetables and the major Mango Mandi in Pernambut — directly supporting local farmers and strengthening the regional agricultural economy.

Entrepreneurship

Business Ventures

Two thriving ventures that drive Pernambut's economy — connecting farmers to markets and fresh produce to homes.

🥕

AAB Vegetables

Fresh Produce · Pernambut

AAB Vegetables is a premier fresh-produce business supplying high-quality vegetables to local markets, households, and retailers across Pernambut. Operating on a farm-to-market model, AAB ensures fresh, nutritious produce reaches every corner of the region daily.

The business directly supports local farmers by offering fair prices, timely procurement, and a reliable supply chain — strengthening the regional agricultural economy.

Fresh Produce Wholesale Farm to Market Pernambut Farmer Support
🥭

Mango Mandi

Agricultural Trade Hub · Pernambut

The Mango Mandi is a major agricultural trade hub in Pernambut — the primary wholesale marketplace for mango farmers across the region. During the season it becomes the heartbeat of local agri-trade, connecting hundreds of growers with buyers across Tamil Nadu.

By establishing a transparent, farmer-friendly marketplace, the Mango Mandi empowers cultivators with better income and wider market access.
